## TMDB identities and corrections

new identities passed the existing strict resolver. Eleven additional decisions were
verified against primary credits, original titles, cast and distinctive synopses;
these include reuse of the canonical FIDOCS *No Money No Honey* and SANFIC *Terra*.
Decisions are agent research, not fabricated human approvals. Previous decisions
for other films remain intact.

The two unresolved films remain visible in Guerrilla 1, with source facts and no
guessed poster or festival history:

- **Corazón danzante, cuando el amor es baile** — Arturo Nicolás Dávila Zelada.
  Title/year, year-free and English-title searches did not corroborate a TMDB work.
  TMDB 1214883 belongs to Amaranta R. Saavedra's different 2023 film; persistently rejected.

  punctuation and English variants did not identify a corroborated work. The
  [distributor](https://prismadistribucion.cl/obras/travesti-travesti-travesti/)
  confirms the film but does not establish a TMDB binding.
